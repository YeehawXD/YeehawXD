# Store copy — NORBERT, UNFINISHED

Draft text for a Steam / itch.io page. Target price **€7.99** (€5.99 launch).
Length: 60–90 minutes. Designed to be finished once, maybe twice, and talked
about afterwards.

## Short description (Steam, 300 char max)

> You are a lump of clay a nine-year-old ran out of time to finish. Tomorrow at
> nine you go in the kiln — and unfinished things explode. Squash, stretch and
> tear off bits of yourself to cross one craft-room table in one night. A short,
> funny, hand-sculpted adventure about not being ready.

## Long description

**Tuesday. 4:58 pm. Craft Club is over.**

A girl called Ivy made you out of one lump of terracotta and forty minutes. She
ran out of both.

You have one long noodle arm and one nub with the armature wire still sticking
out of it. Your left eye is a shirt button. Your right eye is a bead. They do
not match in size, height, or opinion. There is a thumbprint dent in your
forehead where she pressed too hard, and a grey patch on your belly because the
orange ran out, and a little pinched curl on top that she was actually quite
proud of.

You do not have a name.

At nine o'clock tomorrow morning, everything on the drying rack goes into the
kiln, and everything comes out **FINISHED**. Hard. Permanent. Real. It is the
happiest day of your life. Everyone says so.

Unfinished clay has air trapped inside it. Air, at nine hundred degrees, would
very much like to be somewhere else.

You have one night to cross the table.

### YOU ARE MADE OF CLAY, SO USE IT

**Squash flat** to fit under things and hold down heavy plates. Wind up like a
spring and **BOING** six tiles into the air. **Stretch tall** and pour yourself
over ledges no jump can reach. And when none of that works — **tear a piece off
yourself and throw it.** It sticks where it lands. Stand on it. Weigh something
down with it. Jam it in a hole. Then slurp it back.

### THE GAME GETS HARDER BECAUSE YOU WERE KIND

Three times tonight, somebody will need a piece of you more than you do. A leg,
for a pipe-cleaner flamingo who lost hers to the hoover. A plug, for a draining
sink with a friend on the wrong side of it.

Give it away and it is **gone**. Your maximum drops. You get visibly smaller.
Your jump gets weaker. The puzzles get harder. The HUD keeps the empty dents
where those pieces used to be, for the rest of the game.

Nobody makes you do any of it.

### PEOPLE YOU WILL MEET AND NOT FORGET

- **GARY**, a pinecone with two crooked googly eyes and half a pipe-cleaner
  moustache, who is certain he is the Mayor. There was an election. He ran it.
  He was extremely fair.
- **MADAME PIPPA PIPECLEANER**, principal dancer of the Windowsill Ballet for
  one entire glorious afternoon, until the hoover came.
- **STEVE**, who has been fired, and came out wrong, and would like you to look
  at where his handle is. Go on. Look where the handle *is*.
- **GLAZE**, a flawless porcelain swan who would genuinely love to smooth you
  out, fill in that dent, and give you two matching eyes — and who has never
  once been unkind to you, and that is the worst part.
- **BEANS.**
- **THE THUMB.**

### DRAWN OUT OF MATHS, ONE LUMP AT A TIME

Not a single image file. Every character, hillside and thumbprint in this game
is generated at runtime — lumpy polygons run through a hand-built clay shader,
trembling at twelve frames a second the way real stop-motion does, because the
animator kept touching the model between shots.

The squelches are filtered noise. The score is a small music box that improvises
a different key for every room. It all fits in one 255 KB file.

### WHAT YOU GET

- A complete 60–90 minute story with six hand-built chapters
- A clay-morphing puzzle-platformer where your body is the mechanic
- Full controller, keyboard and touch support
- **You cannot die.** You can only come apart a bit, and re-form at the last
  damp pot. This is a cozy game with a kiln in it.
- An ending we are not going to spoil, except to say that nobody explodes

> *Some things aren't meant to be finished.*
> *Some things are meant to be carried around.*
