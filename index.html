    How to play

    The goal

    Both sides have a King Tower and two Princess Towers. Destroy more towers than your opponent before the clock runs out — or take the King Tower for an instant win.

    Elixir

    Elixir refills on its own, up to ten. Every card costs elixir to play, so the real game is spending less than your opponent for the same result. In the last minute elixir flows twice as fast, and in overtime three times as fast.

    Placing critters

    Drag a card onto your half of the arena, or tap the card and then tap the ground. Spells can be cast anywhere. Knock down a Princess Tower and you may deploy on that side of the enemy half too.

    Targeting

    • Ground / Air — some critters cannot hit fliers at all. Always pack an answer to air.
    • Building-seekers like Tusk Charger and Mossy Golem ignore your troops entirely and run straight at a tower. Drop a building to pull them off course.
    • Splash attackers hit everything in a small radius — the counter to swarms.

    Winning trades

    Defend first, then counter-attack with whatever survived. A 3-elixir card that kills a 5-elixir card leaves you two elixir ahead — that gap is how pushes get through.

    Overtime

    Level on crowns when time runs out? Two minutes of sudden death: the next tower to fall decides it. Still level after that, and the lowest tower health loses.

    Controls

    • Drag a card to the arena, or tap-then-tap.
    • 1–4 selects a card, click to place, Esc cancels.
    • Space pauses in practice mode.
