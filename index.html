    How to Play

    The board

    Each side fights from a 3×2 board. The front row takes every melee hit; the back row is untouchable until the front row falls. Put your Guardians where the damage lands and your Marksmen where it does not.

    Elements

    Five elements form one loop, each strong against the next:

    Tide → Ember → Bloom → Stone → Spark → Tide

    Attacking with the advantage deals 1.5× damage and builds ultimate energy 50% faster. Attacking into a resistance deals only 0.7×. Matching elements is about tempo as much as damage.

    Bonds

    Every critter has a Bond — a bonus for standing next to the right ally, or for being in the right row. Bonds are checked against orthogonal neighbours, so moving one critter can change three others. This is where formations are won.

    Ultimates

    Critters gain energy by dealing and taking damage. At full energy their portrait glows — tap it to fire. Holding an ultimate until the right moment is the main decision you make during a fight. Turn on Auto if you would rather watch.

    The run

    Three acts, each a branching map. Health carries between battles, so a cheap win matters. Rest heals, shops sell, elites hurt but pay well. Lose your whole team and the run ends.
